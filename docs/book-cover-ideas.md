# Book cover: ideas (possibility plan, not scheduled)

Owner, Oct 27 2026: "upgrade the book canvas type: optionally edit each part of the cover in its own tab, or mirror/flip the canvas so what I do on the front cover is duplicated or mirrored on the back, or have it work the way it does now; any other features that make book covers easier". Saved as a possibility; nothing here is approved or started.

## What there is today
One canvas the size of the whole wrap (back, spine, front, with bleed), sized from trim, pages and paper by KDP's rules; trim, spine and safe-area guides; a note about spine text under 100 pages; export as a 300 DPI PDF or PNG.

## Ideas, most useful first
1. **Three cover layouts** (one choice in the Book cover group):
   - Whole wrap: as now.
   - Separate parts: Front, Spine and Back each in its own tab (like a stamp's tab beside Canvas), edited at its own size and put together for export; the Canvas tab shows the assembled wrap.
   - Mirrored: design the front; the back follows it as a Copy, a Mirror (left to right) or a Turn (half round). An element can be marked "Front only" (title, author). The spine is its own part.
2. **Spine tools**: a Spine text element that turns and fits the spine width by itself, and is switched off with a note under KDP's 100 pages; a spine colour band.
3. **Print safety**: KDP's barcode box on the back (2 x 1.2 in) as a guide; a warning when text or an element is near the trim, crosses the spine fold or sits under the barcode.
4. **Cover starting points**: layouts with title, subtitle and author on the front, a blurb box on the back and the spine filled in (the box-first text of 2.63.0 makes the blurb easy).
5. **Printer presets**: KDP paperback, KDP hardcover (its wrap margins), IngramSpark, Lulu: each sets the spine sum, bleed and safe margins. A front-only export as an ebook cover (KDP: 1600 x 2560).
6. **Patterns across the wrap**: a pattern runs unbroken across back, spine and front; with Separate parts, on chosen parts only.
7. **Preview**: a simple book mock-up (front and spine at an angle, the fold shaded).

Suggested first release if chosen: 1 and 2.
