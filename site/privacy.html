Wodblok

Privacy

Updated October 2026

Your projects

Projects and files stay on your device. Wodblok does not upload them and cannot see them.

Account

Creating an account stores your email address, first name and password with Clerk, the sign-in service. Wodblok stores your account ID, your account type and whether you are approved.

Usage time

For early-access and tester accounts, the app records the time it was in active use each day, the number of sessions, the app version and the browser name. It records nothing from a project or a file. Other account types are not tracked.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, Wodblok stores the message, its category, the app version and the browser name, and emails the message to the owner.

Waitlist

Earlier visitors who joined the waitlist gave an email address, what they will use Wodblok for and where they heard of it. Ask to be removed at any time.

Cookies and device storage

  • Clerk sets cookies to keep you signed in on this site.
  • When you are approved, a sign-in cookie lets the app open for 24 hours.
  • The app saves its settings, and a seven-day pass for working offline, on your device.
  • The app saves your accent colour as a cookie so this site can use it.

Services used

Clerk (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and database) and Resend (email). Wodblok has no advertising and does not sell data.

Delete your data

Delete your account from Account in the website menu or in the app. This removes your usage, feedback and approval records. To remove a waitlist entry or ask anything else, use the contact link below.
